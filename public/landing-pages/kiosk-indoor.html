Why The Indoor Kiosk

Made for busy indoor spaces.

  • A Small Footprint

    9'5" by 4'5" and 750 kg: it stands against a wall in a lobby, cafeteria or corridor.

  • A Window On The Robot

    A see-through window lets customers watch the robotic arm move their pizza to the oven.

  • Plugs Into A Standard Supply

    208V 20A single phase and a 120V receptacle, with service access from the side.

  • Built To Be Seen

    Robust 16-gauge carbon steel paneling with your full graphic wrap, and LED pot lights under the screen.

How It Works

From tap to hot pizza in minutes.

  1. Tap

    Customers pick a pizza on the 32" touchscreen and add coupon or loyalty codes.

  2. Pay

    Cards, mobile wallets and NFC, plus campus meal plans.

  3. Bake

    A robotic arm moves the pizza to a patented convection oven, where it bakes in its box.

  4. Collect

    Out through the hatch in 3–4 minutes, with a disposable knife on the side.
