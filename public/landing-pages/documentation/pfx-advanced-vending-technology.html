PFX's Advanced Vending Technology

PFX is revolutionizing pizza vending by pairing high-quality ingredients with advanced technology. Our machines are designed to deliver more than just pizza. They provide a fast, seamless and satisfying experience for every customer.

Storage and Selection

The machine’s refrigerated compartment securely stores up to 70 boxed pizzas at a time. When a customer makes a selection, the chosen pizza is seamlessly transferred from cold storage to the baking chamber.

Automated Baking Process

As the selected pizza enters the baking zone, the box is automatically opened and the pizza is gently placed into our advanced pulsed-air convection oven. The oven dynamically adjusts cook time, power, and temperature based on the specific pizza type, ensuring a perfectly baked result every time.

Delivery and Enjoyment

After baking, the pizza emerges hot with a perfectly golden, crispy crust. The entire process, from selection to delivery, is fast and seamless. Customers receive their pizza within minutes, ready for immediate enjoyment or convenient takeaway.

User-Friendly Design: Tailored for the Modern Consumer

This fully automated process showcases both advanced engineering and intuitive design. The PFX machine is a self-contained culinary solution that delivers freshly baked pizza 24/7 with minimal human intervention, perfectly suited for today’s fast-paced, on-the-go lifestyle.

Each PFX kiosk option is uniquely designed to cater to different environmental settings while maintaining high quality and functionality standards. Whether for outdoor, indoor, or ATM-style locations, these units are crafted to deliver the ultimate pizza vending experience.
