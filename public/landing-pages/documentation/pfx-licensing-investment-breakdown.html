PFX's Licensing Investment Breakdown

Launching a PFX unit is truly plug‑and‑play. Our 100% turnkey licensing package eliminates the typical start-up headaches—everything from pizza preparation to payment systems is already handled for you. One price includes it all: equipment, supply chain access, branding, a two-year parts and labor warranty, technical support, and more. Plus, with zero licensing or royalty fees, you keep more of your revenue and reach profitability faster.

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve the PFX experience for our licensees, we’ve made several important updates to simplify the structure, reduce upfront costs, and create a more profitable, scalable model for everyone in the system. Here’s what’s changed in the updated agreement:

  • Simplified Fee Structure: All royalty and advertising fees have been removed.

  • Flat Annual Technology Fee: A single $480-per-unit fee covers 24/7 customer service, SIM/data, remote monitoring, and software updates.

  • Lower Unit Pricing: Standard unit prices are now more than 10% lower.

  • Separated Installation Support: Installation support is no longer built into the unit price. Instead, it’s an optional, low flat fee that covers our travel costs, pay it when you want hands-on setup and training, or waive it for units you can install yourself.

These updates reflect our commitment to making PFX ownership more accessible and profitable for every operator.
