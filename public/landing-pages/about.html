About PFX

Pizza Automation Experts: the white-label division of PizzaForno, bringing automated pizza vending to operators under their own brands.

The PFX Story

Founded on the innovation behind PizzaForno.

Founded on the innovation behind PizzaForno, PFX (Pizza Automation Experts) was established in response to growing demand from pizzerias, foodservice operators, and entrepreneurs seeking automated pizza vending solutions under their own brands.

As PizzaForno expanded across North America, inquiries grew from operators interested in the technology and operational advantages of automated pizza service, but who wanted to retain their own brand identity. Recognizing this market opportunity, PizzaForno developed PFX as its dedicated white-label division, enabling businesses to bring automated pizza solutions to market under their own brands.

Combining PizzaForno’s experience in automated pizza vending with a focus on flexible, scalable solutions, PFX empowers operators to integrate pizza automation into their existing businesses and pursue new foodservice opportunities. Today, PFX extends the reach of pizza automation beyond a single consumer brand, helping businesses leverage proven technology to deliver convenient, on-demand pizza service across a range of markets.
